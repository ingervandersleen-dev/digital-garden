Breathe

Breathe in through your nose for four seconds, hold your breath for four seconds and breathe out slowly for six. Repeat this five times and notice how your shoulders drop and your thoughts slow down.

Five senses

Look at your hand and count your fingers. Name five things you can see, four things you can feel, three things you can hear, two things you can smell and one thing you can taste. It brings your attention back to right now.
